NightShift Pay

Durable payment infrastructure for legal businesses and independent creators. Built for bank and investor partnerships.

The Opportunity

NightShift Pay is a US-based fintech platform in development that provides payment infrastructure for legal businesses and independent creators. We partner with banks and licensed institutions to enable approved card acquiring, NPP/PayTo access, and fast direct payouts (including AUD) to verified recipients.

We do not hold creator or merchant funds. Settlement occurs through our banking partners, with a focus on clear licensing, appropriate reserves, reliable settlement speeds, and robust API integration. Our goal is to build durable, compliant payment pathways for growing segments that have historically had limited access to modern acquiring and local payout options.

Business Model

For Banks & Acquirers Approved acquiring sponsorship, controlled risk, and access to growing legal segments with strong verification and monitoring.
For Creators & Businesses Fast direct payouts, clear fees, and tools that reduce dispute friction without holding funds.
Revenue Platform fees on processed volume plus optional software tools. No fund holding.

Risk Controls

Layered controls designed to reduce chargeback fraud and protect acquiring partners:

  • Seller verification before payouts (identity, business, and bank account ownership)
  • Delivery and access logging for digital goods
  • Real-time monitoring of dispute rates and velocity
  • Targeted reserves on individual sellers when risk rises
  • Clear billing descriptors
  • Chargeback alerts (Ethoca/Verifi) and representment support
